# Go Golf

**A universal golf game. Build a course wherever you want.**

Go Golf lets you build a course anywhere, challenge your skills and compete with friends, all from one bag, with a feel close to real golf. The beach, the park, your backyard or the campus quad: wherever you drop the cup, that's the hole.

Go Golf is a mix of building a hole and daring your friend.

🌐 **Live site:** https://talankreck.github.io/gogolf/

---

## What's in the bag

| | Component | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two-piece adjustable club** | The grip section and clubhead section screw together into one solid club, then come apart to pack. The face adjusts and locks into different lofts. |
| 2 | **Foam balls** | Standard foam balls for everyday play, plus balls with a firmer outer layer for a different feel. |
| 3 | **Portable target** | A circular cup you can put anywhere. |
| 4 | **Breakdown flag and post** | Snaps together so everyone can see the pin, then breaks down small. |
| 5 | **Hitting mat** | A consistent spot to tee off from. |
| 6 | **Carry bag** | Holds the whole game. Grab it and go. |

*Product details are still in development and may change.*

## How a round works

1. **Screw it together.** The two-piece club threads into one solid club.
2. **Set your loft.** Adjust the face and lock it in for the shot you want.
3. **Build the hole.** Drop the target, plant the flag, pick your tee spot.
4. **Play and run it back.** Play 9 or 18. Winner sets the next hole.

## What's on the site

- **The pitch:** the problem (golf is hard to get to) and how Go Golf fixes it
- **What's in the bag:** a detailed look at every component, with a "Pack it up" animation
- **Try a hole:** a playable mini-game where you pick a loft, power up and swing
- **Founding members:** a numbered member card, invite link and recruiter leaderboard *(opening soon)*
- **Trick shot of the week:** upload your craziest shot and vote on the best one *(opening soon)*
- **Dare a friend:** build a hole and send it as a challenge
- **Scorecard:** keep score on your phone while you play (`#scorecard`)
