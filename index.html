The problem

Golf is hard to get to.

A real round takes time, money, a ride and a course. When you can't get there, you miss more than practice. You miss the competition, the pressure shots and playing with your friends.

Tee times

You plan around the course's schedule, not yours.

Cost

Green fees, gear and getting there add up fast.

The course

No course nearby means no golf.

So we made golf you can take anywhere.

The fix

A full game of golf in one bag.

Screw the club together, drop the cup, and the park, the beach or your backyard becomes the course. You choose where the hole goes and what's in the way.

What's in the bag

Everything you need. One bag.

The whole game travels with you: a two-piece club, foam balls, a portable cup, a flag and a hitting mat. Pack it up and take it anywhere.

1

Two-piece adjustable club

The grip section and the clubhead section screw together into one solid club, then come apart to pack. The face adjusts and locks into different lofts.

2

Two kinds of foam balls

Standard foam balls for everyday play, plus balls with a firmer outer layer for a different feel off the club.

3

Portable target

A circular cup you can drop anywhere. Wherever it lands, that's the hole.

4

Breakdown flag and post

Snaps together so everyone can see the pin from the tee, then breaks down small.

5

Hitting mat

A consistent spot to tee off from, on sand, grass or anything in between.

6

The carry bag

A compact golf bag that holds the whole game, organized and protected. Throw it over your shoulder and go.

The adjustable face

One club. Every shot.

Change the loft and the same club plays a low runner, a bump-and-run, an approach or a high shot over whatever's in the way.

A real iron face

Grooves, a sweet spot and a face that adjusts and locks into different lofts. It's built to feel like a real club when you hit it.

Where it started

Golf where you'd never expect it.

We took Go Golf to a beach in Costa Rica. Surfers who had never golfed in their lives walked over and asked if they could play with us.

That keeps happening. When people see golf being played somewhere they'd never imagine it, it's shocking and exciting. And it's not just golfers who want in.

Go Golf is a mix of building a hole and daring your friend.
